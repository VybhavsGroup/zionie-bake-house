The millet promise

Your favourites, reimagined with millets

Zionie Bake House began in a home kitchen with one belief — treats should taste amazing and treat you well. That's why alongside our classic bakes, we craft millet versions of your favourites: ragi brownies, karuppu kavuni (heritage black rice) brownies and whole wheat tea cakes. Calcium-rich, fibre-packed, and honestly? Just as yummy.

  • Baked fresh, made to orderNothing sits on a shelf — your order goes into the oven for the day you need it.
  • Millets without compromiseWe spent months perfecting ragi, karuppu kavuni and wheat bakes that even kids can't tell apart from the classics.
  • We teach what we bakeHands-on classes where you mix, frost and decorate alongside us — ragi secrets included.
