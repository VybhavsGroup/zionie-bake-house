Do I need any baking experience?

Not at all. Beginner classes assume zero experience, and we work the room so nobody gets left behind. The cake studio welcomes everyone from first-timers to hobby bakers.

What's included in the price?

Everything — ingredients, equipment, aprons, printed recipe cards, chai and coffee. You take home everything you bake, plus a Zionie Bake House box.

Can I gift a class?

Yes! Gift vouchers are available for every class and never expire. Mention it in the reservation form and we'll send a beautifully designed voucher by email.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund up to 72 hours before class. Inside 72 hours, we'll happily move you to any future date instead.
